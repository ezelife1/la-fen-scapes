YOUR QUESTIONS

Before we get started.

What work can I enquire about?

Garden maintenance, landscaping, tree work and jet washing for patios, paths and entrance paving. Describe your job so we can confirm its scope and suitability.

Which areas do you cover?

Send your postcode to discuss coverage and availability. A fixed service area has not been supplied for this demo.

What should I send for a quote?

Your postcode, photographs, a description of the job and any access information. Attach photographs in WhatsApp after the form opens.

Can I enquire about regular garden care?

Yes. Tell us whether you need a one-off visit or ongoing maintenance, and we can discuss the work and timing.

Is submitting the form a confirmed booking?

No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. We discuss the work, quote and arrangements before confirming a job.
